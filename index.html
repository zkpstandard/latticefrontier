Program

All times Eastern (EST, UTC−5). Program subject to small changes.

Every session is a prompt talk plus discussion

The designated speaker gives a short, informal talk with slides, 5 to 15 minutes, to frame the topic. The rest of the slot is open discussion.

Venue and Hotels

MIT Stata Center

32 Vassar Street, Cambridge, MA

Building 32G · Room 575

  1. Enter the Stata Center.
  2. Take the elevators for the Gates Tower (the “G” side of building 32).
  3. Go up to the 5th floor and follow signs to room 575.

Logistics

Food

Breakfast, coffee and Monday dinner

Breakfast is provided both mornings; you’ll receive a link to order ahead from a local restaurant. Coffee is served at every break. Lunch is a two-hour break each day, with plenty of options around Kendall Square.

Dinner

Monday, 19:30–23:00 at Batifol

French bistro in Kendall Square, Cambridge. All participants are invited, courtesy of QEDIT. Find it on the map.

Travel support

Flights and hotels

We expect most participants to cover travel from their own research funds. If that isn’t possible for you, contact the organizers and we will help.
